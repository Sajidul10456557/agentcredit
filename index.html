Important Notice & Risks

  • Not an investment. AgentCredit is a one-time Sybil-Proof Verified Credit / Grant check. It offers no income, profit or financial return.
  • Real BNB gas. On BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (Chain ID 56), claiming sends a real transaction and costs real BNB for gas. Blockchain transactions cannot be undone. Use Demo Mode to try it for free.
  • One claim per Agent ID wallet. You need an Agent ID (name.ai) in the connected wallet. The grant amount is set by the contract owner and may be 0.
  • Owner permissions. The contract owner can change the grant amount, pause new claims, withdraw unused funds and transfer ownership. The owner cannot claim for others or edit who has claimed.
  • Hackathon project, not audited. This software has not had a professional security audit. Use at your own risk and only with small amounts.
  • Stay safe. Never share your private key or seed phrase. Always check the transaction in your wallet before confirming. AI agents must never auto-sign: you confirm manually.
