Important Notice & Risks

  • Not an investment. AgentCredit is a one-time Sybil-Proof Verified Credit / Grant check. It offers no income, profit or financial return.
  • Real transaction. A real claim is a transaction on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (Chain ID 56). It may cost a small gas fee in BNB and cannot be undone. Use Demo Mode to try it for free.
  • One claim per Agent ID wallet. Agent ID (name.ai) is non-transferable. The grant amount is set by the contract owner and may be 0.
  • Owner permissions. The contract owner can change the grant amount, pause new claims, withdraw unused funds and transfer ownership. The owner cannot claim for others or edit who has claimed.
  • Hackathon project, not audited. Use at your own risk.
  • Stay safe. Never share your private key or seed phrase. AI agents must never auto-sign: you confirm every transaction manually.
