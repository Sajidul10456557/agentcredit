Why Agent ID?

  • Sybil resistance: one non-transferable Agent ID per user, so one person cannot farm many claims.
  • Agent-native: the whole flow works through an AI agent, with a manual confirmation before any transaction.
  • Transparent: open-source contract on BscScan with owner permissions disclosed.

FAQ

Why can't I connect my wallet here?

Agentic Wallets cannot connect to websites. That is a safety feature. This site only reads the chain, and your agent does the claiming after your manual confirmation.

Does it cost anything?

Checking is free. A real claim is a BNB Smart Chain transaction and needs a small gas fee in BNB.

I am not eligible. Why?

Either the address holds no Agent ID, it has already claimed, or claims are paused. Agent IDs are claimed in the Binance app (Wallet, then the Agent ID banner).

Can I claim twice?

No. The contract allows one claim per Agent ID wallet.

Important Notice & Risks

  • Not an investment. AgentCredit is a one-time Sybil-Proof Verified Credit / Grant check. It offers no income, profit or financial return.
  • Real transaction. A real claim is a transaction on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (Chain ID 56). It may cost a small gas fee in BNB and cannot be undone.
  • One claim per Agent ID wallet. Agent ID (name.ai) is non-transferable. The grant amount is set by the contract owner and may be 0.
  • Owner permissions. The contract owner can change the grant amount, pause new claims, withdraw unused funds and transfer ownership. The owner cannot claim for others or edit who has claimed.
  • Hackathon project, not audited. Use at your own risk.
  • Stay safe. Never share your private key or seed phrase. AI agents must never auto-sign: you confirm every transaction manually.
